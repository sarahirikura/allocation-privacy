Allocation privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026.

Allocation keeps track of your collection: what you paid, retail today, and the resale market. Your collection is private to you. We never sell it, share it, or try to sell your pieces.

What we keep. Your sign-in email (or Apple's relay address). The pieces you add: model, color, purchase date, price paid, condition, documents and notes; a serial number is optional and encrypted. Your boutique ledger, wishlist and alerts. Your community handle, collector card and messages. What you choose to share with a circle. Anonymous offer reports (model, city, months and a spend range, with no link to your account the app can read; shown only when 20 or more members have reported).

What we never keep. Receipt images: a receipt is read once and deleted at once, and card numbers and addresses on it are not kept. Home addresses. Photos of your pieces. We use no analytics, advertising or trackers.

AI. Receipt and photo reading sends the image to Anthropic's Claude API, with nothing identifying you attached. Market estimates come from our research engine, which uses Claude to read public pages about a model, never about you. Every community message is checked by Claude before it posts. Anthropic's API does not train on this data.

Who sees what. Only you see your collection and ledger. Advisor names are private notes, never shown to anyone. A circle sees only the pieces you chose to share, at the level you chose. Other members see your handle, card and posts, never your name or email. Reported messages are reviewed by the app's owner. Our providers, Supabase (database, US), Anthropic, Apple and Expo, see only what they need to run the app.

Values are market estimates, shown with a range and their basis. They are not appraisals, investment advice or guarantees.

Your control. Edit or delete anything in the app. Change your handle, card and sharing at any time. Block and report any member. Delete your account in Settings: it deletes everything above except anonymous offer reports, which can't be traced to you.

Contact: allocationhq@gmail.com. Community reports are answered within 24 hours. If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app tells you first.
